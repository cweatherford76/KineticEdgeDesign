What It Does

One place for everything a small nonprofit runs on.

Most small nonprofits run on a donor spreadsheet, a volunteer sign-up form, a stack of receipts to mail, and a memory of who still needs a thank-you call. The Hub replaces that with a single system that already knows how those pieces fit together.

It was built alongside a working nonprofit, one real request at a time, so it covers what a two-person organization actually does every week instead of what an enterprise sales deck says you should be doing.

What's Included

Everything a small nonprofit needs

Not a stripped-down version of enterprise software. A full platform scoped to how a real nonprofit actually works but can be customized for your needs.

Donor management

The complete record, not just a name and an amount.

  • Contact records with giving history, relationships, VIP flags, and points of contact
  • Duplicate detection when contacts are imported or entered twice
  • One-time and recurring gifts, with net amounts after processor fees
  • Deposit tracking against your bank accounts
  • PDF receipts and year-end tax statements generated automatically
  • Thank-you preferences per donor, so the right people get a letter and the rest get an email

Gifts from where they already happen

Keep the donation tools and fees you already have.

  • Import donations from PayPal, Venmo, Benevity, and Givebutter
  • Bulk-import contacts and gift history from a spreadsheet
  • A branded public donor page that points supporters to wherever you collect payments today
  • The Hub is a CRM, not a payment processor. Nothing changes about how money reaches you.

Campaigns & sponsorships

See what each effort actually brings in.

  • Campaigns with goals, linked gifts, and linked volunteer events
  • Sponsorship levels for business supporters
  • A sponsor portal so sponsors can see their own commitments
  • Announcements to everyone with a portal login

Volunteers

From the first sign-up to the day of the event.

  • Opportunities with named roles, capacity limits, and waitlists
  • Weekly recurring events created in one step
  • Email invites with one-click yes or no, no login required
  • QR-code check-in kiosk for the day of the event
  • Two-stage shift reminder emails
  • Volunteer engagement scoring and a volunteers-who-also-give report

Cultivation & communications

Keep major relationships moving and the writing done.

  • A moves-management pipeline for major-gift prospects
  • Next-action dates with overdue flags so no relationship goes quiet by accident
  • AI-assisted drafts for thank-you emails, appeals, newsletters, formal letters, and social posts
  • Written in your organization's voice from a mission statement you control

Security & control

Your donor data stays yours.

  • Two-factor authentication, with the option to require it for staff
  • Four staff roles: admin, contact manager, cultivation manager, and read-only viewer
  • Full audit log of who changed what
  • Each organization runs on its own isolated database. Your donor data is never in a shared tenant with anyone else's.
